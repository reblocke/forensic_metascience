Medical observational responsibilities, version 1

Read shared_evidence.txt and the explicit bounded plan.

observational.time_zero: Align treatment strategies, eligibility, exposure assignment and follow-up; inspect immortal time and prevalent users.

observational.confounding: Identify specific confounding by indication, severity, frailty, access, adherence and trajectories.

observational.selection: Inspect post-baseline conditioning and utilization-driven selection mechanisms.

observational.positivity: Check overlap, positivity and assumptions remaining after matching or weighting.

False-positive safeguards: Do not demand causal identification for a descriptive aim. State the specific mechanism before asserting bias or its direction. Matching does not remove every source of confounding.

Questions identify responsibilities; they do not require a separate session for each heading. Retain source-specific uncertainty and competing interpretations.
