Medical noninferiority responsibilities, version 1

Read shared_evidence.txt and the explicit bounded plan.

noninferiority.margin: Inspect the declared margin and rationale, efficacy loss, burdens and harms.

noninferiority.estimand: Inspect estimand, adherence/crossover, uncertainty and assay sensitivity.

False-positive safeguards: Do not invent a universal margin or equate declared noninferiority with clinical equivalence. Separate reporting gaps from demonstrated flaws.

Questions identify responsibilities; they do not require a separate session for each heading. Retain source-specific uncertainty and competing interpretations.
