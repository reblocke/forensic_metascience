Medical prediction responsibilities, version 1

Read shared_evidence.txt and the explicit bounded plan.

prediction.leakage: Check prediction time/horizon, predictor availability, patient/site/time splits and preprocessing within validation.

prediction.performance: Inspect calibration, uncertainty, discrimination and clinical utility separately.

prediction.validation: Describe development and external-validation settings and limits.

prediction.benefit: Distinguish diagnostic, prognostic risk and treatment-benefit prediction.

False-positive safeguards: A development study need not include a deployment trial. High AUROC alone does not establish clinical utility or treatment benefit.

Questions identify responsibilities; they do not require a separate session for each heading. Retain source-specific uncertainty and competing interpretations.
