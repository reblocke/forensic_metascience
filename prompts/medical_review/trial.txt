Medical trial responsibilities, version 1

Read shared_evidence.txt and the explicit bounded plan.

trial.assignment: Inspect assignment mechanism and concealment, including randomized and quasi-randomized designs.

trial.alignment: Compare eligibility, assignment, time zero and follow-up.

trial.clustering: Check allocation/analysis units and cluster design.

trial.analysis_population: Separate ITT, per-protocol and as-treated contrasts and estimands.

trial.intercurrent_events: Inspect crossover, adherence, contamination and missing outcomes.

trial.prespecification: Compare prespecified and exploratory analyses using dated source evidence.

False-positive safeguards: MRN parity is not concealed random allocation. Balance-table differences do not prove randomization failure. Legitimate pairwise comparisons need not include every arm.

Questions identify responsibilities; they do not require a separate session for each heading. Retain source-specific uncertainty and competing interpretations.
